The **Profitec Move 2.0** dual-boiler espresso machine now integrates directly with **BeanConqueror**! Elevate your espresso workflow with smart tracking, real-time feedback, and ultimate consistency for every extraction.

### Direct Bluetooth Communication
This integration connects directly via **Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)**. **No Wi-Fi connection is required**, meaning no local network setup, routers, or IP configuration are needed—simply connect directly between your phone or tablet running BeanConqueror and your Profitec Move 2.0.

Ready to connect your machine? Follow our [step-by-step setup and brewing guide](/blog/track-and-control-profitec-move-2/) to get started with brew by weight, preinfusion, and fine-tuning your shots.
