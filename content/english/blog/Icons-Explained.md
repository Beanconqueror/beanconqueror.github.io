When navigating BeanConqueror, you will encounter diverse icons representing brewing stages, preparation methods, and connected hardware. These visual cues are designed to give you instant feedback while pulling an espresso shot or brewing a pour-over, keeping your interface clean, fast, and intuitive.

Here is an overview of key icons and what they mean in your daily workflow.

---

## The Blooming Icon

{{< image src="images/en/blog/icons-explained/beanconqueror-blooming.svg" alt="BeanConqueror Blooming Icon" width="100" height="100">}}

The **Blooming icon**—depicting a roasted coffee bean with rising steam—represents the **blooming (or pre-infusion) phase** of your extraction.

### What is Blooming?
During the initial pour or pre-infusion, hot water comes into contact with freshly ground coffee, causing rapid degassing as trapped carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) is released. Allowing the coffee to bloom before continuing extraction prevents uneven channeling and enhances flavor clarity.

### How BeanConqueror Uses This Icon
In BeanConqueror, you can track blooming parameters precisely:
- **Blooming Time:** Measure the duration of the initial saturation phase.
- **Blooming Water Amount:** Log the exact grams of water added for pre-wetting.
- **Graph Annotation:** When connected to a smart Bluetooth scale, the blooming phase is visualized on your real-time extraction graph.

---

## Preparation Method Icons

BeanConqueror supports every major coffee preparation style, each with a distinct icon:
- **Espresso / Portafilter:** Tailored for 9-bar extractions, lever machines, and smart espresso setups (e.g., Sanremo YOU, Profitec Move 2.0).
- **Pour Over / Filter:** For cone drippers (V60), flat-bottom brewers (Kalita Wave, Origami), and Chemex brews.
- **Immersion:** For French Press, AeroPress, Clever Dripper, and Switch brewers.
- **Cold Brew & Cupping:** For steep-and-filter cold extractions and sensory evaluation sessions.

You can customize which preparation methods appear on your home screen by going to **Menu > Settings > Preparation Methods**.

---

## Hardware & Connection Status Icons

When you integrate smart devices with BeanConqueror, status indicators keep you informed in real time:

- **Bluetooth Connection:** Displays whether your smart scale or pressure transducer is actively streaming weight and flow data.
- **Auto-Tare:** An icon indicating that your connected scale will automatically tare as soon as the timer initiates.
- **Auto-Start / Auto-Stop:** Highlights that automated brew-by-weight logic is engaged to halt your shot or stop the timer when the target yield is reached.
